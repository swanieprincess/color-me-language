# The Learning Dojo: Under the Sea, Part 1 — Fish

- Author: **Swan Moon**. Series: The Learning Dojo, book 2.
- Status: **word list drafted, not yet reviewed**. Art not started.
- Same format as Animals, Volume 1: US Letter with KDP bleed, title page,
  copyright page, 32 fish pages, four corner tags (English, Korean, Japanese,
  Hanja/Chinese in traditional characters), outline lettering to color.

## Draft decisions

- **Saltwater fish only**, to match "Under the Sea". Sharks and rays are fish,
  so they belong here; crustaceans (Part 2) and sea mammals, octopus,
  jellyfish, starfish and sea turtles (Part 3) do not. A starfish or jellyfish
  may still appear as a small background trinket.
- Freshwater favourites left out (swap in if wanted): goldfish 금붕어 / きんぎょ /
  金魚, koi 비단잉어 / こい / 錦鯉, betta 베타 / ベタ / 鬥魚, catfish 메기 /
  なまず / 鯰魚.
- Fish familiar in Korea and Japan were chosen on purpose: saury (さんま /
  꽁치 / 秋刀魚), sea bream (たい / 도미), mackerel, cod, flounder, pufferfish.
- Japanese uses hiragana for everyday food-fish names (ふぐ, まぐろ, さけ,
  さば, いわし, たら, ひらめ, たい, さんま) and katakana for the rest, as in
  Volume 1.

## Words to double-check (`check` column in pages.csv)

These have more than one name in use; confirm with a native speaker:

- Blue tang, zh: 藍倒吊 (Taiwan common name) vs 擬刺尾鯛.
- Angelfish: 神仙魚 / エンゼルフィッシュ usually mean the freshwater angelfish;
  the marine one is 蓋刺魚 / キンチャクダイ. Pick by which fish the art shows.
- Moray eel, zh: 海鱔 vs 裸胸鱔.
- Parrotfish, ko: 파랑비늘돔 is the standard name; 앵무고기 is heard too.
- Sea bream, zh: 真鯛 (in Taiwan 鯛魚 often means tilapia).
- Blobfish: ニュウドウカジカ / 水滴魚 are the usual names; check they read
  naturally to a child.
