# The Learning Dojo: Under the Sea, Volume 1

- Author: **Swan Moon**. Series: The Learning Dojo, book 2.
- Status: **word list drafted, not yet reviewed**. Art not started.
- Same format as Animals, Volume 1: US Letter with KDP bleed, title page,
  copyright page, 32 fish pages, four corner tags (English, Korean, Japanese,
  Hanja/Chinese in traditional characters), outline lettering to color.

## Draft decisions

- **Saltwater fish and sea plants** (Swan, 2026-10-18: "you can use aquatic life
  like flora as well; seaweed is a big one"). 26 fish + 6 sea plants: nori,
  kelp, wakame, hijiki, sea lettuce, seagrass. Sea plants get kawaii faces like
  the animals. Dropped to make room: porcupinefish, boxfish, sailfish,
  barracuda, sardine, manta ray.
- Anchovy (멸치 / かたくちいわし / 鯷魚) added at Swan's request; cod (대구) kept
  too, so sawfish was dropped instead (closest to the stingray).
- Fish are saltwater only, to match "Under the Sea". Sharks and rays are fish,
  so they belong here; crustaceans (Part 2) and sea mammals, octopus,
  jellyfish, starfish and sea turtles (Part 3) do not. A starfish or jellyfish
  may still appear as a small background trinket.
- Freshwater favourites left out (swap in if wanted): goldfish 금붕어 / きんぎょ /
  金魚, koi 비단잉어 / こい / 錦鯉, betta 베타 / ベタ / 鬥魚, catfish 메기 /
  なまず / 鯰魚.
- Fish familiar in Korea and Japan were chosen on purpose: saury (さんま /
  꽁치 / 秋刀魚), sea bream (たい / 도미), mackerel, cod, flounder, pufferfish.
- Japanese uses hiragana for everyday food-fish names (ふぐ, まぐろ, さけ,
  さば, いわし, たら, ひらめ, たい, さんま) and katakana for the rest, as in
  Volume 1.

- **Every fish must have a real name in all four languages** (Swan,
  2026-10-18): no English sounded out in hangeul or katakana. Blue tang
  (블루탱), angelfish (엔젤피시), blobfish (블롭피시) and leafy sea dragon
  (リーフィーシードラゴン) were dropped for this; cutlassfish (갈치 / たちうお /
  帶魚), yellowtail (방어 / ぶり / 鰤魚), remora (빨판상어 / コバンザメ / 鮣魚) took their places.

## Octopus and cuttlefish (Swan, 2026-10-18)

Octopus (문어 / たこ / 章魚) and cuttlefish (갑오징어 / こういか / 墨魚) moved into this
book from the planned "other sea life" part, replacing remora and sea lettuce
(sea lettuce overlapped nori and had uncertain names). The book was then 25 fish,
5 sea plants and 2 mollusks, so the working title is *Under the Sea, Volume 1*
(matching *Animals, Volume 1*) rather than "Fish & Seaweed".

## Theme: sea life from the dinner table (Swan, 2026-10-18)

"We eat them a lot so it just makes sense." The book leans into sea life that
children in Korea and Japan know from home cooking: 김, 미역, 다시마, 톳, 멸치, 고등어,
갈치, 대구, 도미, 문어, 오징어, ふぐ, まぐろ, さんま and so on. Squid (오징어 / いか / 魷魚)
added on that basis, replacing lionfish. Now 24 fish, 5 sea plants, 3 mollusks.

## Familiar to Korean kids (Swan, 2026-10-18)

"I'd replace the ones that are not familiar to Korean kids." Swapped out:
butterflyfish, parrotfish, moray eel, seagrass, swordfish. In: pollock (명태 / すけとうだら /
明太魚), yellow croaker (조기 / キグチ / 黃魚), eel (장어 / うなぎ / 鰻魚), abalone (전복 /
あわび / 鮑魚), jellyfish (해파리 / くらげ / 水母). Now 23 fish, 4 sea plants, 5 other
sea creatures (octopus, cuttlefish, squid, abalone, jellyfish).

- Hammerhead shark dropped (not familiar to Korean kids); starfish (불가사리 / ひとで /
  海星) in its place.
- **Flying fish + its roe are a facing pair** (Swan, 2026-10-18): the flying
  fish (날치 / トビウオ / 飛魚) on a left-hand page and Flying Fish Roe (날치알 / とびこ /
  飛魚卵) on the right-hand page opposite. The only food page in the book.
  Yellowtail dropped to keep 32 pages. Keep the flying fish on an even book page
  if the order ever changes.
- Sea urchin (성게 / うに / 海膽) added, replacing anglerfish.
