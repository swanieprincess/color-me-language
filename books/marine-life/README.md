# The Learning Dojo: Marine Life

- Author: **Swan Moon**. Series: The Learning Dojo, book 2.
- Status: **word list drafted**. Art not started.
- One 32-page book that replaces the two planned Under the Sea volumes (Swan,
  2026-10-25). Renamed from "Under the Sea" because seals, sea lions, walruses,
  sea otters, sea turtles, hermit crabs and seagulls are not strictly underwater.
- Same format as Animals, Volume 1: US Letter with KDP bleed, title page,
  copyright page, 32 animal pages, four corner tags (English, Korean, Japanese,
  Hanja/Chinese in traditional characters), outline lettering to color.
- Full lineup, cut list and overlap check: [docs/marine-life-lineup.md](../../docs/marine-life-lineup.md).
  Korean check for a native speaker: [docs/korean-review-sheet.md](../../docs/korean-review-sheet.md).

## Make-up (32)

- Fish (13): shark, tuna, salmon, mackerel, anchovy, cod, clownfish,
  pufferfish, seahorse, flying fish, eel, anglerfish, stingray.
- Soft sea life (4): octopus, squid, jellyfish, starfish.
- Shells & claws (5): hermit crab, turban shell, blue crab, shrimp, nautilus.
- Whales & dolphins (3): dolphin, orca, blue whale.
- Shore & sea (6): sea turtle, spotted seal, sea lion, walrus, sea otter, seagull.
- Seaweed (1): nori.

## Rules

- A real name in all four languages, no English sounded out in hangeul or
  katakana (so no lobster: Japanese only has ロブスター).
- Familiar to Korean kids, with a few Korean favourites kept on purpose
  (멸치, 고등어, 김).
- No animal in more than one book.
- Hermit crab (page 18) faces turban shell (page 19); keep the hermit crab on
  an even number if the order changes.

Words to double-check are marked in the `check` column of pages.csv.
