# The Learning Dojo: Animals, Volume 1

- Title: **The Learning Dojo** — subtitle *Animals, Volume 1*.
- Author: **Swan Moon**.

- Source of the layout and art: the Moda canvas in the owner's private
  workspace (link kept out of this public repo).
- Pages: 32, see [pages.csv](pages.csv).
- Status: interior built in Inkscape (title page, copyright page, 32 animal
  pages with the four words set in licensed fonts); the source SVG is in the
  art vault as `art/raw/source/learning-dojo-animals-vol1.svg`. Full-wrap cover
  built (`art/raw/source/learning-dojo-animals-vol1-cover.svg`, in the vault):
  front = the title page; back = blurb, a colored sample of the cat's four word
  tags with "Color the words, too!", three star bullets (32 kawaii animals;
  8.5" x 11" pages; names in English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese/Hanja), and the
  author signature centered at the bottom; blank spine (too thin
  for text at 34 pages); KDP barcode area left clear.

## Decisions

- No sea animals in this volume (dolphin removed; turtle redrawn as a garden
  tortoise). Sea life goes into the Under the Sea series.
- Penguin wears a winter beanie and scarf.
- Swan wears a small crown and keeps the black bill knob and mask (mute swan).
- Title page (and the front cover, which reuses it): Swan's colored three-animal
  image on a muted yellow page (#f2e6b0) with colorful stars and sparkles; title
  in neon aqua (#00ffff, edge #0044aa, aqua glow #4fd2ff); "Animals · Volume 1"
  navy (#1f3a6e, edge #00ccff); "Color & learn in 4 languages!" green (#2e6b3a,
  edge #00ff00) with a blue "4"; language names each in their own color. Colors
  taken from Swan's Inkscape export, 2026-10-18. On a black & white interior the
  page prints in grayscale.
- No page numbers (they fell inside KDP's 3/8 in bleed margin).
- Print-ready interior PDF exported by Swan from Inkscape 2026-10-18 (34 pages,
  8.625 x 11.25 in, text as paths, glow rasterized at 300 ppi) is in the vault as
  `art/raw/print/learning-dojo-animals-vol1-interior.pdf`.
- Export flattened; see docs/kdp-checklist.md.
- Words use the everyday native term; see docs/art-direction.md.
