# Series plan

All books share the same page layout and the same four-script word tags.
Each book is themed so a child meets one family of animals at a time.

## 1. Animals, Volume 1 — published

Land animals, birds and freshwater animals. **No animals that live in the
sea.** 32 pages: cat, penguin, koala, deer, panda, lion, monkey, elephant,
zebra, giraffe, rabbit, chick, turtle (garden tortoise), bear, fox, owl, cow,
dog, duck, flamingo, goat, hedgehog, hippo, horse, sheep, mouse, pig, raccoon,
sloth, squirrel, tiger, swan.

Explicitly excluded from this book because they are sea animals: dolphin,
crab, octopus, shark, whale, seal, sea turtle, fish, jellyfish, starfish.
Published on Amazon (2026-10).

## 2. Marine Life — word list drafted

One 32-page book (Swan, 2026-10-25), replacing the two planned Under the Sea
volumes. Renamed because seals, sea lions, walruses, sea otters, sea turtles,
hermit crabs and seagulls are not strictly underwater. Word list:
books/marine-life/pages.csv. Lineup, cut list and overlap check:
docs/marine-life-lineup.md.

## 3. Rivers & Ponds — draft list, under review

Freshwater animals, opening with the river snail 다슬기 (Swan's pick). Frogs, tadpoles, toads, salamanders, the soft-shell
turtle 자라 and the crocodile go here, so there is no separate Amphibians book.
No insects (they get their own book). The draft list is waiting on a check of
how familiar each name is; it is not in the repo yet.

## 4. Insects — planned

Candidates: ladybug, butterfly, bee, dragonfly, ant, grasshopper, beetle,
firefly, caterpillar, moth, praying mantis, cricket, stick insect, cicada,
mosquito (maybe not), stag beetle, rhinoceros beetle, honeybee, bumblebee,
dung beetle, water strider, diving beetle. Spiders and snails are not insects;
decide whether to allow a "bugs" catch-all or keep it strict.

## Not decided

- Reptiles: too few familiar candidates for 32 pages once the water reptiles
  move to Rivers & Ponds. Idea: an Animals, Volume 2 with more land animals and
  a few land reptiles (gecko, chameleon, iguana, snake).

## Decided

- Series title: **The Learning Dojo**. Each book is "The Learning Dojo:
  <subject>", e.g. *The Learning Dojo: Animals, Volume 1*.
- Author: **Swan Moon**, on every cover, title page and
  copyright page.

## Open decisions

- Cover art and back-cover copy.
- Whether to add a copyright page and a "how to say it" pronunciation page.
- Whether to print simplified Chinese alongside traditional for mainland readers.
