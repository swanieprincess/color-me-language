# Series plan

All books share the same page layout and the same four-script word tags.
Each book is themed so a child meets one family of animals at a time.

## 1. Animals, Volume 1 — in progress

Land animals, birds and freshwater animals. **No animals that live in the
sea.** 32 pages: cat, penguin, koala, deer, panda, lion, monkey, elephant,
zebra, giraffe, rabbit, chick, turtle (garden tortoise), bear, fox, owl, cow,
dog, duck, flamingo, goat, hedgehog, hippo, horse, sheep, mouse, pig, raccoon,
sloth, squirrel, tiger, swan.

Explicitly excluded from this book because they are sea animals: dolphin,
crab, octopus, shark, whale, seal, sea turtle, fish, jellyfish, starfish.

## 2–4. Under the Sea — a three-part series

- **Part 1 — Fish.** Candidates: clownfish, goldfish, angelfish, pufferfish,
  seahorse, swordfish, tuna, salmon, betta, koi, flying fish, sunfish,
  lionfish, blue tang, catfish, eel, manta ray, stingray, hammerhead shark,
  great white shark.
- **Part 2 — Crustaceans.** Candidates: crab, hermit crab, lobster, shrimp,
  prawn, krill, barnacle, crayfish, fiddler crab, coconut crab, mantis shrimp,
  spider crab, king crab, pill bug (woodlouse, a land crustacean).
- **Part 3 — Other sea life.** Mammals, molluscs, cnidarians and reptiles:
  dolphin, whale, orca, narwhal, seal, sea lion, walrus, manatee, sea otter,
  octopus, squid, cuttlefish, nautilus, clam, sea snail, jellyfish, starfish,
  sea urchin, sea turtle, sea horse (if not in Part 1), coral, sea anemone.

## 5. Insects — planned

Candidates: ladybug, butterfly, bee, dragonfly, ant, grasshopper, beetle,
firefly, caterpillar, moth, praying mantis, cricket, stick insect, cicada,
mosquito (maybe not), stag beetle, rhinoceros beetle, honeybee, bumblebee,
dung beetle. Spiders and snails are not insects; decide whether to allow a
"bugs" catch-all or keep it strict.

## 6. Amphibians — planned

Candidates: frog, tree frog, poison dart frog, toad, salamander, newt, axolotl,
caecilian, bullfrog, tadpole (life-cycle page), glass frog, fire salamander.

## 7. Reptiles — planned

Candidates: gecko, chameleon, iguana, bearded dragon, tortoise, snake (garter,
corn snake, python), cobra, crocodile, alligator, komodo dragon, skink,
horned lizard, frilled lizard, box turtle. Sea turtles stay in Under the Sea
Part 3. The garden tortoise already appears in Animals Volume 1, so give the
reptile book a different tortoise pose or a box turtle instead.

## Open decisions

- Author name for Volume 1 (series title decided: **The Learning Dojo**; each
  book is "The Learning Dojo: <subject>", e.g. *The Learning Dojo: Animals,
  Volume 1*).
- Cover art and back-cover copy.
- Whether to add a copyright page and a "how to say it" pronunciation page.
- Whether to print simplified Chinese alongside traditional for mainland readers.
