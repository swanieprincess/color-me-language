# Korean word review sheet — Under the Sea, Volumes 1 & 2

For a native Korean speaker to check. Each word should be (1) the normal everyday
name, (2) known to a Korean child, and (3) spelled right. Tick OK, or write a better
word. The pronunciation column is the official romanized spelling, so it is only a
rough guide to the sound.

## Questions for the reviewer

- Do kids say **흰동가리** or 니모? (Clownfish is no longer in the books, but useful for later.)
- **흰고래** or **벨루가** for the beluga? Kids may only know 벨루가 (a loanword).
- Is **갯가재** known to kids, or only to adults who eat it?
- Are **상괭이**, **귀신고래** and **짱뚱어** familiar, or too specialist?
- Anything everyone in Korea knows from the sea that is missing?
